How to Sync

  1. Connect your Figma account inside the Miro Settings.
  2. Open Figma Desktop and ensure the local Figma MCP server is running on port 3845 (required for auto-detect selection).
  3. Select any frame in Figma and click Detect Selection in Miro.
  1. Connect SyncBridge inside the Miro Settings tab.
  2. Copy your unique Pairing ID to the clipboard.
  3. Open the SyncingBoard Companion plugin in your Penpot workspace.
  4. Paste the Pairing ID and click Connect to link them.
